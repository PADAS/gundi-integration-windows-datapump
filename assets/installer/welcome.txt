Welcome to the Gundi Radio Service installer.

This installer registers the Gundi Radio Service as a Windows
Service that reads radio location data from your local dispatch
software's database and forwards it to your configured Gundi or
EarthRanger destination.

The service runs in the background, starts automatically with
Windows, and provides a local web interface at http://localhost:8080/
for status, configuration, and updates.
